# Code of Conduct

We want `chat-sdk-rs` to be a practical, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially across language and timezone boundaries.
- Keep discussion focused on the technical issue and the people affected by it.
- Accept maintainers' decisions on scope, release timing, and project direction.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information or credentials.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests after maintainers have asked to
  move on.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that makes participation unsafe or unproductive, contact a
maintainer privately through the project's GitHub organization. Maintainers will
review reports and may remove comments, close discussions, block users, or take
other action needed to protect the project.
